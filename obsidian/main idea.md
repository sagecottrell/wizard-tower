rogue-lite tower builder

limited amount of time to build a tower before it's destroyed.
you win the game if you can defeat the destruction (and hence preserve the tower).

save game may have multiple towers in progress at once

research tree to enhance various aspects of the tower, and ultimately reach the win condition

tower [[Rooms]] collectively function as a factory. rooms produce resources and send outputs to other rooms for further processing

[[Floors]] of different types can support different kinds of [[Rooms]]
